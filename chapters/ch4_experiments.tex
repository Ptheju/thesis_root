\chapter{Experimental Design and Evaluation Protocol}
\label{ch:experiments}

This chapter details the empirical evaluation harness, hyperparameter grids, and rigorous data hygiene protocols implemented to prevent data leakage.

\section{Benchmark Topologies}
The evaluation framework spans three standard multivariate time series domains from the UEA/UCR repository:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{BasicMotions ($C = 6$):} Low-dimensional motion classification.
    \item \textbf{FingerMovements ($C = 28$):} Sensorimotor EEG rhythms ($\mu/\beta$ band).
    \item \textbf{FaceDetection ($C = 144$):} High-density visual ERP cognitive trials.
\end{itemize}

\section{Downstream Classification Engine}
Following standard time series classification protocols, \texttt{MiniRocket} combined with \texttt{RidgeClassifierCV} serves as the primary feature transformation and classification engine, ensuring computational scalability across thousands of ablation iterations.

\section{Data Hygiene and Prevention of Leakage}
To ensure methodological validity, strict separation of data splits is enforced:
\begin{itemize}
    \item All channel scoring mechanisms (PCA loadings, subspace divergence matrices, and ANOVA $F$-scores) are computed \textbf{strictly and exclusively on the training partition} ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{train}}, \mathbf{y}_{\text{train}}$).
    \item The test partition ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{test}}, \mathbf{y}_{\text{test}}$) remains completely unseen during the channel selection phase.
\end{itemize}

\section{Parametric Sensitivity and Balanced Results Reporting}
To avoid post-hoc reporting bias, results are presented across a multi-tier grid of candidate values for $k$, rather than highlighting isolated single points.
