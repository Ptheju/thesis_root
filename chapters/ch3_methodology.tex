\section{Dimensionality Reduction Methods}
\subsection{Principal Component Analysis (PCA)}
PCA seeks orthogonal directions of maximum variance. Given centered data matrix $X\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times d}$, PCA solves the eigenproblem:
$$X^TX v = \lambda v,$$
where $v$ are principal directions.
